# Ethical Reach and Community Growth

High reach follows usefulness and credibility; do not optimise for viral claims.

## Every day

Post one small proof: problem, stakeholder, screenshot/chart, repository link, one numerical result and one honest limitation.

## Every week

Publish a 60–120 second demo, a technical write-up, one `good first issue`, and a request for a specific type of reviewer such as an ecologist, energy auditor, urban planner, farmer, public-health analyst or NGO operator.

## Every month

Release one polished package or application, run a community review, compare against the prior baseline, publish a changelog and submit useful fixes upstream to libraries or datasets you used.

## Collaboration targets

- local nature and civic groups
- university environmental clubs and labs
- open-source geospatial and scientific Python projects
- public-data publishers
- sustainability startups and NGOs
- citizen-science communities

## Good outreach message

“I built a small open-source tool for **[decision]** using **[data]**. Its current result is **[measured result]**, but I am uncertain about **[specific limitation]**. Could you review **[one concrete assumption or workflow]**?”
