# Impact Measurement Guide

## The evidence ladder

1. **Output:** code, dashboard, dataset or alert exists.
2. **Use:** a real stakeholder used it in a decision or workflow.
3. **Outcome:** behaviour, allocation, reliability, cost, emissions proxy, exposure or ecological monitoring changed.
4. **Impact:** a credible comparison supports attribution of a meaningful social or environmental benefit.

Do not jump from output to impact in your README.

## Required `IMPACT.md` fields

- Stakeholder and decision
- Geographic and temporal scope
- Primary metric and unit
- Baseline and comparison method
- Result with absolute and relative change
- Uncertainty and sensitivity
- Distributional effects: who gains, who may lose, who is missing
- Environmental rebound or unintended consequences
- Data and model limitations
- Verification owner and next validation date

## Suggested evaluation designs

Use the strongest practical design: before/after with controls, interrupted time series, matched comparison, randomised rollout, expert adjudication, backtesting, simulation with clearly stated assumptions, or prospective shadow mode before operational use.
